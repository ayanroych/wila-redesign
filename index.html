Our Foundation

Built on Four Defining Principles

Rooted in the Haas Defining Leadership Principles, WILA channels these values into action for women.

✨

Question the Status Quo

Diversity & Inclusion — Challenging norms and championing equitable representation.

💖

Confidence Without Attitude

Positive Engagement & Well-Being — Leading with humility and authenticity.

📚

Student Always

Professional Development — Continuously growing through learning and mentorship.

🌎

Beyond Yourself

Community Support & Impact — Creating positive change that extends beyond ourselves.
